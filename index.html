THE HUNT - HOW TO PLAY

  • Shoot moving targets by pressing FIRE when the reticle is over the animal.
  • You have limited AMMO. Each shot costs ammo, bears cost extra.
  • Missing or running out of ammo ends the hunt.
  • Each animal rewards food (meat) if hit.
  • Try to maximize food before running out of ammo or time!
Music will play when you start. Sound required for best experience.
